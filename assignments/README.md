## Student workflow

1. Find your assigned task in the list above and read its complete instructions.
2. Check the applicable course group, deadline, collaboration rules, required files, and evaluation criteria.
3. Follow the [contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md) to synchronize your fork and create a branch.
4. Put your solution in the DS3 or DS4 destination specified by the task. Keep instructions in this folder and solutions in the student folders.
5. Run the required checks and include the assignment identifier and a link to its instructions in your pull request.
6. Respond to review on the same branch until the contribution is accepted.

Ask the instructor about unclear requirements before implementing assumptions. Students must not edit task instructions unless explicitly authorized.

## Publishing a task — instructor

Copy `TEMPLATE.md` to `assignments/TASK-ID/README.md`, replacing `TASK-ID` with a stable identifier such as `exercise-01`, `lab-01`, or `project-01`. These are naming examples, not published assignments. Complete every placeholder, mark the task Published, and add a link under Published tasks above.

A task folder may also contain starter files, sample inputs, or supporting documents referenced from its README. Use one task for both course groups when requirements match; state group-specific deadlines or requirements explicitly when they differ. Keep the identifier stable after publication. Record substantive changes in the task's change history and notify students through the course channel.
